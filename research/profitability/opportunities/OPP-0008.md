# OPP-0008 — Perpetual funding carry (long spot / short perpetual)

- rank: **REJECT** · evidence score: **27/50** · status: `REJECTED_INSTRUMENT_AND_CAPITAL_FIT`

- assets: BTC, ETH

## Economic hypothesis

Perpetual futures have no expiry, so the venue pays a funding rate to keep the contract tethered to spot. When funding is positive and persistent, holding spot against a short perpetual harvests that payment while staying delta-neutral: the carry is a structural transfer from leveraged longs to liquidity providers.
