# OPP-0006 — Turn-of-the-candle intraday anomaly in Bitcoin returns

- rank: **REJECT** · evidence score: **19/50** · status: `REJECTED_COST_FLOOR`
- sources: S-06
- market: Bitcoin spot (multiple venues in the source, including Bitfinex)
- assets: BTC
- timeframe: minute-level

## Economic hypothesis

Exchange candle boundaries are a coordination device for order flow. A measurable, recurring drift appears around the turn of the candle, which is claimed to be statistically and economically significant and to survive an out-of-sample test.

## Rules as stated by the source (nothing invented)

- entry: PARTIAL: the anomaly is defined as returns around the turn of the candle; the source does not readably state a tradable entry rule at the level read.
- exit: UNKNOWN
- risk: UNKNOWN
- parameters: The candle-turn window (source uses venue-relative timing); no thresholds, no sizing.

## Claimed evidence

- period: in-sample historical sample plus an out-of-sample window January-August 2022 (Bitfinex)
- metrics: positive returns of about 0.58 bps per minute around the candle turn, described as statistically and economically significant
- transaction cost model: NONE
- external code: NONE
- external results: Peer-reviewed anomaly paper with an explicit out-of-sample test; unusually good for this registry.
- forward evidence: NONE · live evidence: NONE

## Required data

minute-level exchange data (public archives)

## Counter-evidence

- 0.58 bps per minute is an order of magnitude below the project's 23.791 bps round-trip cost floor; the whole anomaly is smaller than one side of the trade.
- The anomaly is tied to exchange-specific candle timing, so its magnitude is sensitive to which venue's clock defines the turn - a fidelity trap for replication.
- No cost model, no net-of-cost result, and no statement about what share of the 0.58 bps survives a market-order entry.

## Ambiguities

- Whether the return is capturable at all with a market order at the boundary is untested by the source.
- The exact window length and pre/post split are not established at the level read.

## Risk classification

- replication difficulty: MEDIUM methodologically, but the result is economically unusable.
- evidence quality: HIGH as an anomaly, LOW as an opportunity.
- selection bias: LOW · overfitting: MEDIUM (many minute windows are testable) · lookahead: LOW · implementation: HIGH (sub-minute execution)
